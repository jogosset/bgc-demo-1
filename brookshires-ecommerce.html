Our story

Four generations. One hometown.

In 1928, Wood T. Brookshire opened a 2,500-square-foot grocery on the courthouse square in Tyler, Texas. His sons Bruce and Woody grew it store by store; their kids kept the name on the door and the lights on late for anyone who needed a loaf of bread on a Sunday.

We're still family owned. Still stocking the shelves with what grows nearby. Still the place where the butcher knows your name and the produce guy will tell you which peaches to buy today, not tomorrow.
